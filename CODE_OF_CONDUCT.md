# Code of conduct

Xyne Island welcomes contributors who collaborate with care, curiosity, and
respect.

Participants must:

- be considerate across differences in experience, identity, background, and
  communication style
- critique ideas and code without personal attacks
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and
  publication of private data
- respect security embargoes, licenses, and the privacy of Spaces content that
  appears in screenshots, logs, or reproduction steps

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or participation that
violate these expectations. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented
privately, and free of conflicts of interest.

Report conduct concerns through the private channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include sensitive personal details in a
public issue. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably
possible and will not retaliate against good-faith reports.
